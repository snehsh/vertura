01 — Flagship

The Vertura Foundation Programme

In person in Pune, ages 10–14.

Eighteen months across five levels. The full build — from how a child reasons, through how they perform and lead, to shipping something real in front of people who do this for a living.

  • Duration18 months, five levels.
  • FormatWeekends, roughly three hours per session, in person.
  • GroupMaximum 20 students, mentored at 20:1.
  • Next cohortDates being confirmed. The waitlist is open now.

02 — Online

Vertura AI Labs

Live online, ages 13–16.

A lab, not a course. Four weeks in which students think, research, create, build and solve with AI, ending in a showcase where they present what they made and explain how they got there.

  • Duration4 weeks, twice a week, live online.
  • CohortsAges 13–14 at 4:00 PM, 15–16 at 5:30 PM, three batch options each.
