5 core outcomes

Everything in the four weeks builds towards these five. Tap any one to see what students actually do.

Students learn to think critically with AI — treating it as a thinking partner instead of an answer machine.

  • Asking better questions
  • Prompting intelligently
  • Understanding where AI falls short
  • Evaluating AI-generated information
  • Deciding when not to accept the answer

Students learn to investigate a question properly instead of stopping at the first result.

  • Researching topics in depth
  • Comparing opposing perspectives
  • Analysing information
  • Finding patterns across sources
  • Exploring complex questions

Students turn ideas into things they can see, share and be proud of.

  • AI-generated images and visuals
  • Written content and stories
  • Presentations
  • Creative concepts of their own

Students move past generating content and start combining AI tools and ideas into something useful.

  • Taking an idea from thought to working output
  • Using more than one tool together
  • Testing, breaking and improving what they made
  • Finishing with: "I had an idea. I actually built it."

Students take on real-world challenges and use AI to work through them.

  • Identifying the actual problem
  • Breaking it into parts
  • Exploring possible solutions
  • Experimenting with AI
  • Presenting their thinking to others
Minimum theory. Maximum hands-on.

This is not a class where students just watch.

Every session is designed to get students actively doing something. This is the shape of the four weeks.

  1. Learn

    A short, sharp idea — then straight into the work.

  2. Experiment

    Hands on the tools, testing what they can and can't do.

  3. Create

    Turning an idea into an output they can show.

  4. Build

    Combining tools and ideas into something that works.

  5. Solve

    Taking on a challenge with no single right answer.

  6. Showcase

    Presenting the work, and the thinking behind it.

It doesn't end with a lesson.
It ends with a showcase.

At the end of the four weeks, every student gets the chance to present what they explored, created and built — and to explain how they got there.

For most students, it's the first time they've stood up and defended an idea that was entirely their own.

Questions parents ask

Is prior AI knowledge required?

No. Students can join without any prior AI experience. The first sessions assume nothing.

Is this a coding course?

No. This is a hands-on AI learning experience focused on thinking, researching, creating, building and solving with modern AI tools. There is no programming syllabus.

Are the sessions live?

Yes. Every session is conducted live online with the Vertura team. Nothing is pre-recorded.

What if my child misses a session?

Every session is recorded. If your child misses one, the recording is shared with enrolled parents so they can catch up before the next session. Because the program is hands-on, we'll also flag anything they need to complete to stay with the group.

Will students receive a certificate?

Yes. Students who complete the program receive a Certificate of Completion from The Vertura Academy.

What device does my child need?

A laptop or computer with a stable internet connection is recommended for the best hands-on experience. A tablet will work for watching, but not for building.

How many sessions are conducted every week?

Two sessions a week, for four weeks. Each session is one hour, on the two days you choose at enrollment.

Can I speak to someone before enrolling?

Yes, and we'd prefer it. Message us on WhatsApp at +91 90223 17329 and you'll speak to the Vertura team directly, not a call centre.
