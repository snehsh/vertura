The VerturaGrowth Index

A clear picture of how each student is growing across six core competencies, scored at the start, middle and end of every 12-week level, and based on what they actually do in sessions, not on a single test day.

See the sample report Collaborate with us
What it measures
Six core competencies, built from 60 behavioural indicators, each scored on a 1 to 5 behavioural scale.
How often
At Week 1, Week 6 and Week 12 of every level, so you see the change, not just a final mark.
Who receives it
Parents, or the collaborating school for students in Vertura for Schools.
What it is based on
Worksheets, recordings, projects and the facilitator's notes from every session.

Facilitator narrative

Student A has moved from accepting explanations at face value to actively hunting for the assumption underneath them. His Week 11 SWOT stood out for naming a genuine weakness, inconsistent revision habits, with specific dated evidence rather than a vague self-description. The next stretch for him is catching cognitive biases in the moment, not just when the class discussion points there.

Why schools use it

It gives you something specific to share at parent meetings: how each student has moved in each capability, with the work behind the score.

Each competency is scored against the student's own Week 1 baseline. It rewards real progress, so a quiet child who has grown a lot is recognised just as clearly as a confident one.
