The six verticals.

Schools commonly weight the earlier grades towards communication, leadership and self-mastery, and the later grades towards critical thinking, financial literacy and AI. The selection is yours, and it usually follows what your parent body is already asking you about.

01

Communication

Structured speaking, persuasive writing, and being understood the first time.

02

Leadership

Negotiation, judgement under pressure, leading a group without holding a title.

03

Self-Mastery

Habit systems, deep work, and emotional regulation under real friction.

04

Financial Literacy

How money grows, how businesses make it, and how to read cost, risk and return.

05

AI & Technology

Using modern tools to think, research, create and build — not just to answer.

06

Critical Thinking

Mental models, first-principles reasoning, and catching your own bias.

How a partnership runs.

  1. 1

    A conversation with the school

    What your parent body is asking for, where the gaps sit, and which grades you want covered first.

  2. 2

    You choose the verticals per grade

    Not every grade needs all six. The mapping is agreed with you rather than handed to you.

  3. 3

    It runs in a timetabled period

    Delivered inside the school week by Vertura, using the same four-step session method as every other programme.

  4. 4

    You get the Growth Index each quarter

    Every student read across six capabilities. Something specific to show at a parent meeting instead of a line about holistic development.

What it does not touch
Subject teaching. No syllabus coverage, no exam preparation, no interference with academic periods.
Who delivers it
Vertura, inside your building, to your timetable.

What the school gets to show.

The Vertura Growth Index scores six measured dimensions per student — cognitive growth, discipline consistency, communication development, leadership behaviour, project quality, and the mentor's own observations.

It goes to the school each quarter. It replaces the certificate that every programme hands out and that tells a parent nothing.
