    What school leaders say.

    The six verticals.

    Schools commonly weight the earlier grades towards communication, leadership and self-mastery, and the later grades towards critical thinking, financial literacy and AI. The selection is yours, and it usually follows what your parent body is already asking you about.

    01

    Communication

    Structured speaking, persuasive writing, and being understood the first time.

    02

    Leadership

    Negotiation, judgement under pressure, leading a group without holding a title.

    03

    Self-Mastery

    Habit systems, deep work, and emotional regulation under real friction.

    04

    Financial Literacy

    How money grows, how businesses make it, and how to read cost, risk and return.

    05

    AI & Technology

    Using modern tools to think, research, create and build, not just to answer.

    06

    Critical Thinking

    Mental models, first-principles reasoning, and catching your own bias.

    How a collaboration runs.

    1. 1

      A conversation with the school

      What your parent body is asking for, where the gaps sit, and which grades you want covered first.

    2. 2

      You choose the verticals per grade

      Not every grade needs all six. The mapping is agreed with you rather than handed to you.

    3. 3

      It runs in a timetabled period

      Delivered inside the school week by Vertura, using the same four-step session method as every other programme.

    4. 4

      You get the Growth Index each quarter

      Every student is assessed across six capabilities, giving you something specific and meaningful to share at parent meetings.

    What it does not touch
    Subject teaching. We don't cover the syllabus or prepare students for exams, and academic periods stay exactly as they are.
    Who delivers it
    Vertura faculty, inside your building, to your timetable.
    Class size
    Your existing sections, each led by a Vertura faculty member.

    What the school gets to show.

    The Vertura Growth Index scores six measured dimensions per student: cognitive growth, discipline consistency, communication development, leadership behaviour, project quality, and the mentor's own observations.

    It goes to the school each quarter, and it shows parents real, specific progress in a way a participation certificate can't.

    See the full sample report
    How it is scored
    An overall score out of 100, and six competencies each scored from 1 to 5 against the student's own Week 1 baseline.
    What it is based on
    60 behavioural indicators, observed in worksheets, recordings, projects and the facilitator's notes.

    Questions principals ask first.

    Does Vertura take time away from subject teaching?

    No. It runs in one timetabled period a week that the school sets aside, and it covers no syllabus. Academic periods stay exactly as they are.

    Who teaches the sessions?

    Vertura's own faculty, inside your school and on your timetable. Each section is led by a Vertura faculty member, so your teachers don't need to prepare or deliver anything.

    Which grades and verticals can we choose?

    Any of the six verticals, for any grade from 5 to 10. Most schools choose two verticals per grade, and we recommend a mix based on what your parents are already asking for.

    How does it fit around exams and holidays?

    We plan the calendar with you before the term starts. Sessions pause during exam weeks and major holidays, so students are never pulled away from exam preparation.

    How are students assessed, and what does the school receive?

    Every quarter, the school receives a Vertura Growth Index report for each student, scored across six capabilities and based on the work they produced in sessions. See a sample report.

    What does the school need to provide?

    A weekly period in the timetable and a classroom for each section. We bring the session plans, worksheets and everything else needed to run the session.

    How is it priced?

    Pricing is per student per year and depends on the grades and verticals you choose. We share a written proposal after our first conversation.

    How do we get started?

    It starts with a short conversation with you. Most schools then begin with an eight-week trial term before moving to a yearly collaboration.

    Still have a question?
    Ask it directly in the enquiry form, or message us on WhatsApp. It comes to us, not to a call centre.
